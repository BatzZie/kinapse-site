Privacy policy

Kinapse · last updated 29 October 2026

Who we are

Kinapse is a private app run by Ivar Waleveld for his own family (the Netherlands). It is only used by family members who choose to connect their own accounts. Contact: ivarwaleveld@gmail.com.

What data Kinapse accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): messages in the connected family member's own mailbox, to find appointments, tasks and things that need follow-up.
  • Google Calendar: reading the connected family member's calendars to avoid conflicts, and creating events only in a separate calendar that Kinapse creates itself ("Kinapse").

Kinapse never sends email, never deletes or changes existing mail or calendar items, and never acts on someone's behalf without their explicit approval.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is only used to provide Kinapse's features to the family member it belongs to (and, where that person agrees, to the rest of the family).
  • Data is stored on the family's own private server, which is not reachable from the public internet. Access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • To understand messages, short excerpts may be processed by an AI model (Anthropic Claude). They are not used to train AI models.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Kinapse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Any family member can disconnect Kinapse at any time via Google Account > Third-party connections. On request, all data Kinapse holds about that person is deleted.

Privacyverklaring

Kinapse is een privé-app van Ivar Waleveld voor zijn eigen gezin. Alleen gezinsleden die zelf hun account koppelen gebruiken het.

  • Gmail (alleen lezen): om afspraken, taken en dingen die opvolging nodig hebben te vinden.
  • Google Agenda: lezen om overlap te voorkomen; afspraken maken alleen in een eigen agenda "Kinapse".
  • Kinapse verstuurt nooit mail, verwijdert of wijzigt niets en doet niets namens iemand zonder diens toestemming.
  • Gegevens staan op de eigen server van het gezin, niet bereikbaar vanaf internet. Korte stukjes tekst kunnen door een AI-model (Anthropic Claude) worden verwerkt, niet om AI te trainen.
  • Gegevens worden nooit verkocht, niet voor reclame gebruikt en met niemand anders gedeeld.
  • Koppeling verbreken kan altijd via Google-account > Verbindingen met derden; op verzoek worden alle gegevens verwijderd.
