Kinapse

kin (family) + synapse: a private family assistant.

Kinapse is a private, self-hosted app used by one family (the Waleveld family, the Netherlands). It reads the family members' own mail, calendars and school messages, turns them into tasks, calendar items and reminders, and sends notifications to the family members.

Kinapse is not a public service. It is not offered to, or available for, anyone outside the family.
